Privacy & Data Policy

Your data, explained plainly.

No legal jargon. Here's exactly what we collect, how it's protected, and what you can do with it.

What we collect

Account details: Your name and email address — used to identify your account and nothing else.

Learning activity: Your stage progress, chat conversations with the AI tutor, assessment answers, AI feedback, and your personalized summary. This data powers the personalized experience you see inside the platform.

Passwords: Never stored in plain text. Passwords are hashed before storage — we cannot read them.

How it's protected

Your data is stored securely and is only ever visible to your own account — no other student can see your progress, chat history, or answers.

Access to your account is gated by authentication; we use per-account data isolation so that querying for data from one account cannot return data belonging to another.

How AI personalization works

Our AI tutor and assessment system treat every student equally — everyone receives the same questions and is graded against the same standard.

Anything the AI learns about topics you found difficult is used only to personalize your own summary and review content. This information is:

  — Never shared with other students
  — Never shared with instructors
  — Never used to restrict what content you can access

Your learning struggles are yours alone, and they only ever work in your favour.

Data retention

Your data stays tied to your account for as long as you use the platform. You can request complete deletion at any time — your account and all associated data (progress, chat history, assessment results, personalized summary) will be permanently removed.

Want to delete your account and all your data?

To delete your account: Sign in, open your account settings, and select "Delete account". This action is irreversible — all your progress, chat history, assessment results, and personalized summary will be permanently erased.

If you no longer have access to your account, contact the project administrator directly.

A note on project scope

This is a student project built for an academic tinkering lab. It follows genuine security practices — secure authentication and per-account data isolation — but has not undergone the additional hardening (rate limiting, formal data retention policy, legal review) required for a public production launch.

We're being transparent about this because we think honesty matters more than sounding more official than we are.
